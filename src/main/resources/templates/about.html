About Project

Modern HRMS for smart organizations

HR Synergy is a modern Human Resource Management System designed to simplify and digitalize HR operations. It helps organizations manage employees, attendance, leave, payroll, recruitment, events, feedback and enquiries.

The system reduces manual paperwork, improves data accuracy, increases HR efficiency and provides a centralized platform for daily HR activities.
